WhatsApp Order Bot

A bot that takes food orders over WhatsApp — understands "2 jollof and 1 coke", handles typos, builds a basket, collects delivery details and confirms the order.

This is the real conversation engine, running in a simulator so you can try it without a WhatsApp account. No LLM — a customer should get the same answer every time and never be charged for a hallucinated item.
